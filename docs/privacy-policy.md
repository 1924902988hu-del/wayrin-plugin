## 1. Who we are
Wayrin ("we") operates https://wayrin.com and the Wayrin MCP server at https://wayrin.com/mcp. Contact: [contact email].

## 2. What we collect
- **Account data**: when you sign in with X (Twitter), your X user id, handle, display name and avatar. If you give us an email address, we store it to send account notices.
- **Content you submit**: works and drafts you create, including files or conversation excerpts you explicitly choose to send through the MCP tools `prepare_configurations` and `prepare_conversation`. Drafts are private until you publish them yourself on wayrin.com.
- **Connection data**: OAuth client registrations and access grants for AI tools you connect, and the last time a connection was used.
- **Usage data**: counts of reads of public works (at most one per user per work per day), follows, and basic request logs (IP address, user agent, time) kept for security and rate limiting.

We do not read files on your computer. The AI tool decides what to send, and our tools only accept content you chose.

## 3. How we use it
To run the service (sign-in, show and store works, connect AI tools), to show creators how often their works are used (aggregated), to prevent abuse (rate limits, security), and to send service emails.

## 4. Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We use processors to run the service: Cloudflare (hosting, database), Resend (email), and X (sign-in). Public works and your public profile are visible to everyone. We disclose data if required by law.

## 5. Retention
Account data and content are kept while your account exists. Drafts you delete are removed. Request logs are kept for up to [N] days. After you delete your account we delete or anonymize your data within [N] days, except where law requires us to keep it.

## 6. Your choices
You can revoke any AI connection under Start sharing on wayrin.com, delete drafts and works, and ask us to export or delete your data at [contact email].

## 7. Children
Wayrin is not intended for children under 13 [or the age required in your country].
